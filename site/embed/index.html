Set your own starting numbers

Add values to the end of the address to prefill the fields. For example, ?a=25000&r=0.5&m=50 sets a $25,000 account, 0.5% risk and a $50 point value, which suits E-mini S&P 500 futures. The keys are a account, r risk %, e entry, s stop, m multiplier, t target and theme=dark.
