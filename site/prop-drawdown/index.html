How it works

A trailing drawdown follows your highest balance up. Your liquidation level is highest balance - max drawdown, and it rises as you make new highs but never falls back.

Many firms stop the trailing once the level reaches your starting balance, and some stop it at starting balance plus $100. Pick the option that matches your firm. Firms also differ on whether the high is measured intraday on open profits or at end of day, so check the exact rule before relying on this number.

End of day or intraday

End-of-day firms (Topstep, Tradeify, Lucid, Take Profit Trader tests, Apex EOD accounts, MyFundedFutures evaluations) move the floor only from your closing balance, so enter your highest close. Intraday accounts (Apex Intraday, MyFundedFutures Rapid funded, Take Profit Trader PRO) follow your peak balance including open profit, so giving back an open winner still raises the floor. Every firm here checks the floor in real time, open losses included.

Worked example

Apex 50K Intraday Performance Account: your peak was $51,200 and you are at $50,800. The floor is $51,200 - $2,000 = $49,200, so you have $1,600 of room. It stops rising at $50,100 once your peak reaches $52,100. Open this example →

FAQ

What is a trailing drawdown? A loss limit that follows your highest balance up by a fixed distance and never moves down. Your liquidation level is your highest balance minus the max drawdown, until the firm's lock point.

When does the trailing drawdown stop moving? It depends on the firm and stage. Topstep and Take Profit Trader tests lock at the starting balance. Apex Performance Accounts, Tradeify funded accounts and LucidFlex lock at starting balance + $100. Apex evaluations on Tradovate and Tradeify evaluations never lock.

Do open losses count against the drawdown? Yes, at every firm preset here. Even end-of-day drawdowns are enforced in real time, so an open losing trade that touches the floor fails or closes the account.

What balance should I enter as my highest balance? For end-of-day accounts, your highest closing balance. For intraday accounts, the highest your balance reached at any moment, including open profit.
