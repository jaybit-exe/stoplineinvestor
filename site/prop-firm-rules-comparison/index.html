What the table shows

Exact-threshold behavior is where traders get caught. Topstep, MyFundedFutures, Tradeify and Lucid count a best day exactly at the limit as passing. Apex and Take Profit Trader say "less than" or "below", so you need a little more profit than the simple math shows. The consistency calculator handles both: its firm presets set the rule for you.

Every drawdown here is enforced in real time. Even the end-of-day firms compute the floor at the close but liquidate you the moment your balance, open trades included, touches it. Where the floor stops matters as much as its size: Topstep and Take Profit Trader lock at your starting balance, Apex Performance Accounts, Tradeify funded accounts and LucidFlex lock at starting balance plus $100, and Tradeify evaluations and Apex Tradovate evaluations never lock. The trailing drawdown calculator has a preset for each.

Daily loss limits vary the most. Apex EOD evaluations have a fixed one, Topstep and Lucid let you opt in, and the Tradeify Select evaluation, MyFundedFutures Rapid and the Take Profit Trader Classic and Intraday tests have none. Take Profit Trader's EOD plan has a $1,000 limit on 50K. Where a firm offers one, it pauses trading for the day rather than failing the account, except that Take Profit Trader's EOD plan fails the account if you hit the limit while below your starting balance.

Topstep Challenges, a separate product

Checked Nov 24, 2026 against Topstep's Challenges article and the Maximum Loss Limit article. Challenges are not a 50K evaluation, so they are not a row in the table above, and the Topstep Combine presets in the calculators do not apply to them. They also do not replace the Trading Combine.

You pay a one-time fee, start at a $0 balance, and have to hit the same profit target twice against a static Maximum Loss Limit that does not trail. There is no daily loss limit and no consistency target. Each round lasts up to 90 days. After the fixed payout (you keep 100% of it), the account closes. See our Topstep rules guide for the official size table, or the Nov 24, 2026 news article.

FAQ

Which prop firms count hitting the consistency limit exactly as passing? Topstep, MyFundedFutures, Tradeify and Lucid Trading count a best day exactly at the limit as meeting the rule. Apex Trader Funding and Take Profit Trader require your best day to be below the limit.

Which futures prop firms have no daily loss limit? On the 50K evaluations compared here: Tradeify Select, MyFundedFutures Rapid and the Take Profit Trader Classic and Intraday tests have none. Topstep and LucidFlex make it optional, and Apex EOD evaluations and the Take Profit Trader EOD plan have a $1,000 limit on 50K.

Which firms use an end-of-day trailing drawdown? All six firms use an end-of-day drawdown on the 50K plans compared here, enforced in real time. Apex also sells intraday accounts, and MyFundedFutures Rapid funded accounts, Take Profit Trader Classic PRO accounts and every stage of its Intraday plan trail intraday.

Is this comparison a paid ranking? No. No firm pays to be listed or placed, and the page has no affiliate links.
