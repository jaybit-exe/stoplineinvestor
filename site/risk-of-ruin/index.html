Run 500 simulated trading runs to see how often your plan blows up.

How it works

Each run plays your win rate, reward-to-risk and risk size over the number of trades you choose, and stops if the account falls by the ruin amount. The survival rate is the share of 500 runs that never hit it.

Losing streaks happen even with a real edge. Try cutting risk per trade from 2% to 1% and watch survival jump. Results are estimates from your inputs, not predictions.

FAQ

What is risk of ruin? The chance that a series of losing trades takes your account down by a set amount, given your win rate, reward-to-risk and risk per trade.

How does the simulator work? It plays 500 random runs of your plan for the number of trades you choose and counts how many never fall by your ruin amount. Each click runs a new set, so results move a little.

How do I lower my risk of ruin? Risk less per trade. Cutting risk per trade usually improves survival more than any small change to win rate.
