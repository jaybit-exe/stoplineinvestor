About Stopline

Why Stopline exists

Most trading calculators are cluttered, slow or built to sell you something. Stopline is a set of clean, free risk tools for traders who want to plan a trade before they place it: how big, where to stop, what it needs to win, and how much room an account has. There is no signup for any tool.

How the calculators work

Every calculator uses standard, published formulas, and each page shows the formula and a worked example. The numbers you enter are processed in your browser. They are not sent to a server. Progress such as streaks and journal entries is stored in your own browser.

What these tools are not

Stopline is educational. It does not know your full situation, it does not model slippage, taxes or fees unless a tool says so, and results are only as good as the inputs. Nothing here is financial, investment or legal advice, and trading involves risk of loss. Read the terms and disclaimer.

Who makes it

I'm Jamie, a disabled U.S. Army veteran. I served as an intelligence analyst, and I have traded stocks, perpetual futures and crypto since 2018. I built Stopline to bring an analyst's discipline to trading tools: verify the source, date every rule, and size the risk before anything else.

Stopline is an independent project. It is not owned by, paid by or affiliated with any prop firm, broker or exchange.

How prop firm rules are checked

Every prop firm guide and calculator preset is taken from the firm's own help center or rules page, never from forums or other review sites. Each guide shows the date its rules were last checked and links to the exact page used. When a firm's wording is unclear or two of its pages disagree, the guide says so instead of guessing, and points you to the firm's support.

Prop firms change their rules often. Guides are re-checked against the source pages regularly, and the "Updated" date at the top of each guide changes when they are. If you spot a rule that has changed before we do, please tell us.

How the calculators are built

Each calculator uses a standard formula, shows it on the page, and includes a worked example you can check by hand. Firm presets only fill in the inputs; the math is the same for every firm.

Money and independence

Stopline is free and has no paid placements. If affiliate links are ever added, they will be clearly labelled on the page where they appear and listed in the terms, and they will not change how rules are described.

Corrections and feedback

If a calculation looks wrong, a rule is out of date or a page is unclear, email stoplineinvestor@outlook.com or use the contact page. We review formulas and rules and update pages when we find mistakes.
