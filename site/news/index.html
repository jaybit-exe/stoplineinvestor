How we cover news

  • We start from the firm. Every item cites the firm's own help center, announcement or terms page, with the date we checked it.
  • We do not copy articles. We link to reports that add context and write our own explanation of what changed.
  • If we cannot verify it, we leave it out. Rumors and unconfirmed reports do not run.
  • Corrections are public. When something changes or we get it wrong, we fix the page and say so.
