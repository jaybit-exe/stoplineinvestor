Blue Guardian Futures Zero: Daily Payouts, and an $8,000 Daily Profit Limit You Should Know About

New programPublished Nov 19, 2026Checked Nov 19, 2026

Published Nov 19, 2026. Checked against Blue Guardian's announcement (Nov 17, 2026) and its Zero account rules help article (shown as updated Nov 15, 2026) on Nov 19, 2026. Plan terms change, so read the current Zero rules before you buy. Stopline is not affiliated with Blue Guardian, and this is not an endorsement. Blue Guardian is not one of our firm presets yet, so the calculator links below use Custom values.

What changed

Blue Guardian's announcement of Nov 17, 2026 introduces Futures Zero, a one-phase futures evaluation on NinjaTrader with a 6% profit target, sizes from $25K to $150K, and no monthly or activation fees. Per its rules article:

  • Payouts: every day once your balance clears a per-size buffer. No payout cap, $500 minimum, 90% profit split, by crypto or Rise. Requests are still reviewed, and Blue Guardian says they are processed within 24 business hours.
  • Evaluation options, chosen at checkout: end-of-day or intraday trailing drawdown, with or without a daily loss limit, and a 30%, 40% or 50% consistency rule. They stay with the account for life.
  • Consistency: evaluation only. Going over the limit is not a breach; you keep trading until your best day fits. Funded accounts have no consistency rule.
  • Funded drawdown: always intraday trailing, and it stops trailing at your starting balance plus $100.

The clause that matters most

Every funded Zero account has a maximum daily profit of $8,000, whatever its size. If an account makes $8,000 net profit in a single trading day, it is closed, and Blue Guardian emails two options: take a $3,600 payout per account that hit it, then wait out a 30-day cooldown before buying a new evaluation, or move to a Live account with a Live Bonus. Profit above $8,000 that day is not kept. Also note that news trading is allowed in the evaluation but not on funded accounts, and accounts close after 10 days without a trade.

What it means for your numbers

Consistency is the evaluation's main trap. Blue Guardian's own example: on $50K with a 40% rule and a $2,000 best day, you need at least $5,000 total profit to pass. The consistency calculator shows the same result for any best day.

The funded drawdown trails your highest equity, open profit included, until it locks. On a $50K account the loss limit starts at $48,000 and locks at $50,100 once equity reaches $52,100. Use the drawdown calculator with "Starting balance + $100" to see your room at any equity level.

The $8,000 line means a funded account should size so that a great day does not end the account unplanned. That is a decision, not a default, so work out your contract count first.
