Topstep Stops New Accounts in 13 Countries: What Happens to Combines and Funded Accounts

RestrictionPublished Nov 19, 2026Checked Nov 19, 2026

Published Nov 19, 2026. Checked against Topstep's help center on Nov 19, 2026. Topstep can update this page again, so confirm the current wording on Topstep's eligibility article before you act. Stopline is not affiliated with Topstep.

What changed

On Nov 17, 2026 Topstep updated its list of ineligible countries. Effective that day, Topstep no longer offers new accounts or purchases to traders whose account address is in Andorra, Cambodia, Ethiopia, Guinea, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Macao, Mozambique, Namibia, Senegal, Serbia, Uzbekistan or Zimbabwe. New purchases and profile edits are blocked for those traders.

Topstep calls it a business decision and says it saw a sharp rise in coordinated abuse from several countries. Closing those accounts did not stop it, because the same activity returned under new profiles. In a Nov 16 post on X, quoted by Finance Magnates, Topstep said the change affects fewer than 1,000 current traders.

Account typeWhat happens, per Topstep
Express Funded, Live Funded, and Challenge or Labs drops that reached the funded stageKeep trading, requesting payouts and getting called up to Live under the usual rules, before and after Dec 31. Back2Funded is not available, and if a funded account closes after Dec 31 a new one cannot be started.
Trading CombineThe subscription is cancelled and will not rebill. You can trade until your paid-through date or 5 p.m. CT on Thu, Dec 31, 2026, whichever comes first. No extension, and it cannot be restarted. Banked Resets still work; new ones cannot be bought.
Express Funded Account activationNormally there is no deadline. For traders in these countries, activation closes at 5 p.m. CT on Dec 31, 2026, and Topstep says this includes Trading Combines passed before Nov 17.
Everyone elseNo change announced to trading rules, drawdown or consistency.

What it means for your numbers

If you live in one of the 13 countries and are still in a Combine, the clock is the real rule now. From Nov 19 to Dec 31 there are about 29 trading days once you leave out Thanksgiving (Nov 26) and Dec 25, and fewer if your paid-through date comes sooner.
