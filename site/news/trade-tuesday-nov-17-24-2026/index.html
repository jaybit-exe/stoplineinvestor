Trade Tuesday, Nov 17–24, 2026: The VIX Touched 40, Then Stocks Won Back Half the Drop

Trade TuesdayPublished Nov 24, 2026Checked Nov 24, 2026

Trade Tuesday covers Tuesday to Tuesday. This edition runs from Tuesday Nov 17's close to about 10:15 AM ET on Tuesday Nov 24. Published Nov 24, 2026. Analysis, not a forecast or advice.

One of the most violent weeks in years: a three-day slide into Friday, a 40 print on the VIX, Bitcoin at a two-year low on Saturday, and then the biggest one-day rally since April 2025 on Monday. Here is what the numbers say and what they mean for size.

What moved

Into Friday. From Tuesday's close to Friday's, ES fell 5.8% and NQ 7.2%. On Friday alone the S&P 500 dropped 3.70% to 6,910.39 and the Nasdaq Composite 5.28% to 22,617.73, putting the S&P in a correction, more than 10% below its Oct 6 high (Schwab, AP). It was the S&P's seventh straight loss, its longest streak since February 2020, and Nvidia fell 14% on the day (Schwab). The drivers Schwab lists: doubts about how the AI buildout gets financed, Anthropic's slide after its record IPO, Jensen Huang's sudden departure from Nvidia, chip-export worries, and hawkish Fed talk. By late Friday, CME FedWatch priced 92% odds of a December rate hike (Schwab). The VIX touched 40.03 intraday and closed at 35.89.

The weekend. Bitcoin kept falling after stocks closed, to about $55,100 early Saturday, its weakest level since September 2024, before bouncing back above $60,000. CoinDesk, citing CoinGlass, put the liquidations at about $1.8 billion, mostly long bets.

Monday. Nvidia's board reinstated Huang as CEO, and Nvidia rose 31%. The S&P 500 gained 4.73% to 7,237.17 and the Nasdaq 6.51% to 24,089.56, both their best days since April 2025 (CNBC), recovering just over half of the prior week's loss (AP). Bitcoin did not join in: it ended Monday about where it started, near $61,200.

Tuesday morning. The Conference Board's consumer confidence index jumped to 103.3 from 91.9. At about 10 AM ET, ES was near 7,230 and Bitcoin near $59,900.

What is next

Wednesday, Nov 25 is the busy day: PCE inflation, the second Q3 GDP estimate and durable goods at 8:30 AM ET, new home sales at 10:00 AM and the Beige Book at 2:00 PM. With the market pricing a December hike, PCE is the print we expect to matter. Thursday is Thanksgiving: US stock exchanges are closed, and CME equity-index futures halt at 1:00 PM ET. Friday is a short session: stocks close at 1:00 PM ET and ES and NQ at 1:15 PM ET. Next Tuesday, Dec 1, brings ISM manufacturing and JOLTS at 10:00 AM. Every date and time, with its source, is on the week-ahead calendar.

What this does to your size

Ranges are wider than they were a week ago. The 14-day average true range (ATR) on ES rose from 167 points on Nov 17 to 211 on Nov 23, up 27%. On NQ it rose from 1,013 to 1,179, up 16%. The VIX is an annualized implied move, so divide it by √252 (about 15.9) to get the daily move options are pricing: about 1.7% at 27.3, against 1.2% at 19.3 two weeks ago.

The rule stays the same: fix your dollar risk and let the stop breathe with volatility. Example, using a stop of one quarter of the implied daily move as a stand-in for a structural stop:

On a prop account, the trailing drawdown matters more than the stop. A $2,000 drawdown (the size on many 50K evaluations) is 40 points on one ES contract, under a fifth of the current 211-point ATR. On MES it is 400 points. In a week like this one, mini contracts on a small drawdown can breach on noise alone. Check your room in the trailing drawdown calculator before the 8:30 AM print, then size in the position size calculator (point values are on the futures P&L page).

Holiday sessions: thin books can mean wider spreads and slippage past your stop. Smaller size, and be flat well before Friday's early close. Your prop firm's flat-by time may be earlier than the exchange's.
