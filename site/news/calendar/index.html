Economic Calendar: Nov 25 to Dec 1, 2026

Weekly calendarPublished Nov 24, 2026Dates and times checked Nov 24, 2026

The week ahead, Wednesday to Tuesday, to go with this week's Trade Tuesday. The US releases that can move ES, NQ and Bitcoin, in Eastern Time, with how we would size around them.

The week at a glance

  • Wednesday, Nov 25 is the day that matters. PCE inflation, the second GDP estimate and durable goods all print at 8:30 AM ET, jobless claims are expected in the same minute, new home sales follow at 10:00 AM and the Beige Book at 2:00 PM.
  • Thursday is Thanksgiving and Friday is a shortened session, so liquidity is the risk, not data.
  • Next Monday and Tuesday start December: Chicago PMI Monday, then ISM manufacturing, JOLTS and construction spending Tuesday at 10:00 AM.
  • Not in this window: no CPI or jobs report. The November jobs report is Friday, Dec 4, and CPI is Thursday, Dec 10 (BLS). The next FOMC meeting is Dec 8-9.

How to read the ratings: ES / NQ and BTC show how much we expect each release to widen ranges. They say nothing about direction. They are Stopline's editorial judgment, not a forecast, and a surprise can move any of them.

How to size around it

Keep your dollar risk fixed and let the stop do the adjusting. A wider stop means fewer contracts, not the same contracts with more risk. Ranges are wide right now: ES's 14-day average true range was 211 points on Nov 23, up 27% in a week (see Trade Tuesday).

Example: $50,000 account, 1% risk ($500), Micro Nasdaq (MNQ, $2 per point)StopRisk per contractContracts
Normal session20 pts$4012
Wed, Nov 25, 8:30 AM, if you need a stop twice as wide40 pts$806
  • Before a high-impact print: decide in advance whether you are flat, reduced or full size. Do not decide in the first minute.
  • On a prop account: your trailing drawdown is the real limit, so check your room first. Some firms restrict trading around news. Topstep cuts ES and NQ to 0 in most Challenges during Tier 1 events (see our post), and Blue Guardian does not allow news trading on funded Zero accounts. Check your own firm.
  • Holiday sessions: thin books can mean wider spreads and slippage past your stop. Use smaller size, and be flat before the early close.

Holiday hours

US stock exchanges are closed on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov 26, and close at 1:00 PM ET on Friday, Nov 27 (1:15 PM for eligible options) (NYSE). CME lists Nov 26-27 as its Thanksgiving holiday, and day orders entered after a product's pre-open on Wednesday, Nov 25 are for the Friday, Nov 27 trade date (CME trading hours). For ES, NQ and the micros, CME's schedule as published by futures brokers shows a halt at 12:00 PM CT (1:00 PM ET) on Thursday, a reopen at 5:00 PM CT (6:00 PM ET), and an early close at 12:15 PM CT (1:15 PM ET) on Friday (Lincoln Park Financial, from CME's table). Confirm the exact times on CME's site and with your prop firm, which may flatten accounts earlier.
