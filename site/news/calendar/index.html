Economic Calendar: Week of Nov 23, 2026

Weekly calendarPublished Nov 19, 2026Dates and times checked Nov 19, 2026

The US releases that can move ES, NQ and Bitcoin this week, in Eastern Time, with how we would size around them. A short, holiday-shortened week.

The week at a glance

  • Wednesday, Nov 25 is the day that matters. PCE inflation, the second GDP estimate and jobless claims all print at 8:30 AM ET, and the Beige Book follows at 2:00 PM.
  • Thursday is Thanksgiving and Friday is a shortened session, so liquidity is the risk, not data.
  • Not this week: no CPI, PPI or jobs report. The BLS schedule shows the November jobs report on Friday, Dec 4. The Fed already released its October meeting minutes on Nov 18, and the next FOMC meeting is Dec 8-9.

How to read the ratings: ES / NQ and BTC show how much we expect each release to widen ranges. They say nothing about direction. They are Stopline's editorial judgment, not a forecast, and a surprise can move any of them.

How to size around it

The cleanest rule is to keep your dollar risk fixed and let the stop do the adjusting. A wider stop means fewer contracts, not the same contracts with more risk.

Example: $50,000 account, 1% risk ($500), Micro Nasdaq (MNQ, $2 per point)StopRisk per contractContracts
Normal session20 pts$4012
Wed, Nov 25, 8:30 AM, if you need a stop twice as wide40 pts$806
  • Before a high-impact print: decide in advance whether you are flat, reduced or full size. Do not decide in the first minute.
  • On a prop account: your trailing drawdown is the real limit, so check your room first. Some firms also restrict news trading. For example, Blue Guardian does not allow it on funded Zero accounts (see our post). Check your own firm.
  • Holiday sessions: thin books can mean wider spreads and slippage past your stop. Use smaller size, and be flat before the early close.

Holiday hours

US stock exchanges are closed on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov 26, and close at 1:00 PM ET on Friday, Nov 27 (NYSE). CME lists a Thanksgiving holiday schedule for Nov 26-27, and day orders entered after Wednesday evening's pre-open are for the Friday, Nov 27 trade date (CME trading hours). For ES, NQ and the micros, futures brokers publishing CME's schedule list a halt at 12:00 PM CT (1:00 PM ET) on Thursday, a reopen at 5:00 PM CT (6:00 PM ET), and an early close at 12:15 PM CT (1:15 PM ET) on Friday. Confirm the exact times on CME's site and with your prop firm, which may flatten accounts earlier.
