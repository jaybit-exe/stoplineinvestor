Topstep Makes Challenges Permanent: Hit the Target Twice for a Fixed Payout. The Math Behind It

New programPublished Nov 24, 2026Checked Nov 24, 2026

Published Nov 24, 2026. Checked against Topstep's help center on Nov 24, 2026. Confirm the current terms on Topstep's Challenges article before you buy. Stopline is not affiliated with Topstep. Challenges are separate from the Trading Combine, so our Topstep Combine presets do not apply to them.

What changed

Challenges were first sold only through Topstep Labs. Topstep now says they are a permanent part of its lineup. Prop Desk logged the change on Nov 23. How they work: pay once, hit the profit target in a Challenge Round, activate the free Payout Round and hit the same target again, then request a fixed, one-time payout and keep 100% of it. The account closes after the payout. A Challenge does not lead to a funded account.

Rules for both rounds: the balance starts at $0, the Maximum Loss Limit is static (it does not trail), there is no daily loss limit, no consistency target and no resets, and each round lasts up to 90 days. You can buy up to 5 of each Challenge a month and hold up to 5 in the Payout Round, separate from the 5 Express Funded Account limit. A $10 Starter Challenge ($100 target and payout, $100 max loss, 1 micro, 2 free resets) is only for traders who have never bought from Topstep.

News events cut your size to zero in most Challenges. Per Topstep, during Tier 1 News Events ES and NQ go to 0 contracts in the $500 to $9K Challenges, and MES and MNQ are cut to 0 in the $500 Challenge and to 1, 3, 6 or 9 lots in the larger ones. What it means for your numbers

A static loss limit and a fixed target make Challenges unusually easy to model. Our own simplification: if price moves like a fair coin, with no edge, no gaps and no costs, the chance of reaching +target before -max loss in one round is max loss / (target + max loss). You have to do it twice.

ChallengeTarget / max lossPass one round, no edgePass bothExpected payoutFee
$5002.5 : 128.6%8.2%$41$49
$1.5K3 : 125%6.25%$94$99
$3K3 : 125%6.25%$188$199
$6K3 : 125%6.25%$375$399
$9K3 : 125%6.25%$563$599

So a trader with no edge loses about 5% to 16% of the fee on average, and needs to pass each round about 26% of the time to break even on the $3K (the square root of $199 / $3,000). In plain terms, Challenges are priced close to fair for a coin-flipper. Any real edge, or discipline that a static floor rewards, tilts the math your way, and commissions, slippage and news-event limits tilt it back. Treat this as a rough guide, not a prediction: real markets gap, and your edge is the unknown.

How to size for it: with a static floor, the full max loss is yours from the first trade. On the $3K Challenge, $1,000 is 20 points on one ES contract, or 200 points on MES. If you risk $100 a trade, you have 10 losers before the account ends and need 30 winners' worth of $100 to hit $3,000, twice.
