Drawdown recovery

How much you need to gain to climb back after a loss.

Results

How it works

Losses hurt more than gains help. Down 10% you need 11.1% to recover, down 20% you need 25%, and down 50% you need 100%. The formula is 1 / (1 - drawdown) - 1.

That is why cutting risk per trade is the cheapest way to stay in the game.

FAQ

Why does a loss need a bigger gain to recover? The gain is measured from a smaller balance. After a 20% loss, $100 becomes $80, and $80 needs to grow 25% to get back to $100.

What is the formula for drawdown recovery? Gain needed = 1 / (1 - drawdown) - 1. A 50% drawdown needs a 100% gain.

How many 1% losses make a 20% drawdown? 23 in a row, because each loss is 1% of a slightly smaller balance.
