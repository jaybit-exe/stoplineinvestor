Futures P&L

Points, ticks and dollars for popular futures contracts.

Results

How it works

Futures P&L is points moved times the contract point value times the number of contracts. For ES one point is $50 and one 0.25 tick is $12.50. Micros (MES, MNQ) are one tenth of the size.

Always confirm contract specs and fees with your broker or exchange, since they can change.

FAQ

What is the difference between a point and a tick? A point is a whole unit of price. A tick is the smallest move the contract allows: 0.25 points on ES, MES, NQ and MNQ, 0.01 on CL and 0.10 on GC.

How much is one point worth on ES and MES? $50 per point on the E-mini S&P 500 (ES) and $5 on the Micro E-mini (MES), so one tick is $12.50 and $1.25.

How much is one point worth on NQ and MNQ? $20 per point on the E-mini Nasdaq-100 (NQ) and $2 on the Micro (MNQ), so one tick is $5 and $0.50.

Does this include commissions and fees? No. The result is the gross price move times the point value. Subtract your broker's commissions and exchange fees for net profit.
