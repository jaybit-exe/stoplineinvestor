Apex Trader Funding Trailing Drawdown Rules (2026)

Updated Nov 24, 2026. Rules re-checked Nov 24, 2026 against Apex's help center: EOD evaluations, EOD drawdown explained, intraday drawdown explained, daily loss limit explained, 50% consistency requirement, EOD payouts and Intraday PA payouts. They apply to Apex's current accounts. Apex says its new rules apply to new products only, so older "legacy" accounts can follow different rules. Confirm on Apex's help center before you trade. Stopline is not affiliated with Apex.

The short version

You pick an EOD Trail or an Intraday Trail account, hit the profit target without touching the trailing drawdown, and move on to a Performance Account (PA). Here is the 50K EOD Trail plan as listed on Apex's EOD evaluations page (checked Nov 24, 2026):

Rule50K EOD Trail evaluation
Starting balance$50,000
Profit target$3,000
Max drawdown$2,000, end of day
Daily loss limit$1,000
Max contracts6
Minimum days to pass1
Evaluation window30 days, no resets

Once you pass, Apex gives a 7-day window to activate the matching EOD Performance Account (EOD Evaluations, checked Nov 24, 2026).

EOD trailing drawdown

On EOD accounts, Apex recalculates your threshold once a day at 4:59:59 PM ET from your closing balance. It follows your highest closing balance and never moves down. The new level then applies in real time for the whole next session, so touching it intraday liquidates the account. In an evaluation that is a fail. In a PA the account is closed (Apex EOD drawdown explained).

Intraday trailing drawdown

Intraday Trail accounts use the same $2,000 distance on a 50K, but the threshold follows your peak balance in real time, open profit included. Apex's example: an open trade lifts a $50,000 account to $50,900, so the threshold jumps to $48,900 straight away, even if you close the trade at $50,300 (Apex intraday drawdown explained). This is stricter than EOD, because giving back open profit still moves your floor up.

Where the trailing stops

This is the detail most traders get wrong, and it depends on your account and platform (Apex):

  • Performance Accounts: the threshold stops at starting balance plus $100. On a 50K PA it locks at $50,100 once your high reaches $52,100.
  • Tradovate evaluations: the threshold keeps trailing with no stop.
  • Rithmic and WealthCharts evaluations: the threshold stops at the profit target balance, $53,000 on a 50K. Since you pass at $53,000, in practice it trails for the whole evaluation.

In the calculator, choose "Starting balance + $100" for a PA and "Never" for an evaluation. For EOD accounts enter your highest closing balance. For Intraday accounts enter your highest balance at any moment, including open profit.

Daily loss limit

EOD evaluations have a fixed daily loss limit: $500 on a 25K, $1,000 on a 50K, $1,500 on a 100K and $2,000 on a 150K. Intraday evaluations have none. The limit counts open losses, and hitting it closes your positions and pauses trading until the next session, which starts at 6 PM ET. It does not fail the account (Apex daily loss limit explained).

In a PA the daily loss limit and contract limit grow with your profit:

The 50% consistency rule for payouts

Apex's 50K plan lists no consistency rule for the evaluation. In a PA, your largest profitable day must be less than 50% of your net profit since your last payout before you can request one. The quick math is highest day / 0.5. Apex's example: a $1,500 best day means you need at least $3,000 in net profit. Being over the line does not fail the account. You keep trading until the share drops, and the count resets after each approved payout (Apex 50% consistency requirement).

Apex says "less than" 50%, so landing exactly on the line should not count. There is one wrinkle: the example table on the same Apex page marks a day at exactly 50% ($1,000 of $2,000) as "Consistency Met". The text and the table disagree, so treat exactly 50% as a fail and aim a little above the total the calculator shows. If it matters for a payout, ask Apex support before you request it.

PA payouts (current accounts)

These figures are for current Performance Accounts, not legacy PAs and not live accounts. They were copied from Apex EOD payouts and Apex Intraday PA payouts, both checked Nov 24, 2026.

Approved PA payouts are issued at a 100% split: you receive 100% of the approved payout amount. The minimum request is $500. Each Performance Account may receive a maximum of six payouts; after the sixth, Apex says the PA is closed and you would need to qualify through another evaluation. The 50% consistency rule applies to the profit since your last approved payout (see the section above for the official wording conflict at exactly 50%).

You need at least five qualifying trading days before a payout, not necessarily consecutive. A qualifying day must meet a minimum daily net profit that differs by plan on the 50K, 100K and 150K sizes:

Account sizeEOD PA min daily profitIntraday PA min daily profitSafety netMin balance to request
25K$100$100$26,100$26,600
50K$250$200$52,100$52,600
100K$300$250$103,100$103,600
150K$350$300$154,100$154,600

Apex defines the safety net as the drawdown limit plus $100, and says only profit above the safety net is eligible. The "minimum balance to request" already includes the safety net and the $500 minimum. There is no deadline to finish the five qualifying days while the account stays active.

EOD PAs also have a per-payout cap that steps up by payout number. On a 50K EOD PA the caps are $1,500, $1,500, $2,000, $2,500, $2,500 and $3,000 for payouts 1 through 6 (EOD payouts, checked Nov 24, 2026). On a 50K Intraday PA the caps are $1,500, $2,000, $2,500, $2,500, $3,000 and $3,000 for payouts 1 through 6 (Intraday PA payouts, checked Nov 24, 2026).

What these Apex pages do not state, so this page does not either: a processing time for simulated PA payouts (the live-program FAQ mentions 48 hours for live payouts, which is a different product), a payout schedule more specific than "up to weekly" after five qualifying days, or a single min-daily-profit figure that covers both EOD and Intraday 50K accounts (they differ: $250 vs $200). Live accounts are also different: the live FAQ describes a 90/10 split and a $3,100 safety net, which should not be mixed into the PA numbers above. Legacy PA rules (including a 30% consistency rule on some legacy accounts) are a separate help-center section and are not used here.

FAQ

Does Apex have a daily loss limit? Yes, on EOD evaluations: $500, $1,000, $1,500 and $2,000 on the 25K, 50K, 100K and 150K, according to Apex's EOD evaluations page (checked Nov 24, 2026). Hitting it pauses trading for the rest of the session and does not fail the evaluation. Apex says Intraday evaluations have no daily loss limit.

Does hitting the consistency rule fail the account? No. Apex's 50% consistency page (checked Nov 24, 2026) says the account stays active and the payout request is held until the share falls below 50%. Evaluations have no consistency rule.

Does exactly 50% pass? Apex's own pages disagree with each other. The consistency article and both current PA payout pages say a best day must be less than 50%, and that 50% or more is not enough. The example table on the same consistency page marks exactly 50% ($1,000 of $2,000) as met. This guide treats exactly 50% as not enough and says to confirm with Apex before a payout that lands on the line.

Does an Apex evaluation trail forever on Tradovate? On EOD accounts, yes: EOD drawdown explained (checked Nov 24, 2026) says Tradovate evaluations trail indefinitely with the peak EOD balance. Rithmic and WealthCharts evaluations stop trailing at the profit-target balance. Performance Accounts lock at starting balance plus $100.

What is the difference between EOD and intraday trailing? EOD trailing is recalculated once at the close (Apex states 4:59:59 PM ET) and then enforced live the next session. Intraday trailing follows the peak balance during the session, including open profit. Both can liquidate you the moment the live balance touches the threshold.

A simple plan for a 50K EOD account

  • Treat the $1,000 daily loss limit as your real daily risk, and the trailing threshold as your account risk. Size from whichever is smaller with the position size calculator.
  • In the evaluation there is no consistency rule, but in the PA a single big day can hold up payouts. Keep days steady from the start.
  • Pace the 30-day window with the profit target pace calculator.
