The official formulas

On a Trading Combine, Topstep's check is best day profit ÷ total profit. That share must stay at or below 55%. There is no rounding and no buffer. You can pass in as few as two days if the profit target is also met. The same 55% applies to every Combine size; only the dollar amounts change (Topstep, checked Nov 24, 2026).

Account sizeProfit targetBest day recommendation
$50K$3,000Less than $1,650
$100K$6,000Less than $3,300
$150K$9,000Less than $4,950

Those dollar figures are 55% of each profit target. Topstep labels them "less than," and its Combine parameters page says the best day must stay below 55%. The consistency article, which this calculator follows, says at or below 55%. If you land on the line, confirm with Topstep.

What happens if you go over on the Combine

Going over does not fail the account. It raises your profit target to best day ÷ 0.55. You then have to reach that new total without any single day exceeding the best day. Losing days do not reset the best day. Combine best days lock at 3:10 PM CT. Extra profit on the same session adds to that best day, so the remaining profit has to come on a later trading day.

The 40% Express Funded Consistency path

When you activate an Express Funded Account (XFA), you choose Standard or Consistency as the payout path. That choice cannot be changed later (Topstep).

On the Consistency path, the formula is largest single-day net profit ÷ total net profit. The result must be 40% or below, with no rounding, and you also need at least three trading days with at least one trade per day. Exactly 40% qualifies. If you are over 40%, keep trading on smaller days so the share comes down. After a payout request, the calculation resets to $0 and applies only to profits earned in the new window, which starts the next trading day. The day you request a payout does not count toward the next three. The dashboard figure locks at 4:00 PM CT (consistency article and payout policy, checked Nov 24, 2026).

The 40% objective is the same on every XFA size this page could confirm. The Standard path is different: five winning days of $150 or more, and no consistency objective. This calculator is not for the Standard path, for Live Funded Account payouts, or for Topstep Challenges. For the rest of the Combine and XFA rules, see the Topstep rules guide.

FAQ

How is Topstep's 55% Combine consistency target calculated? Topstep's formula is best day profit divided by total profit. On a Trading Combine, that share must stay at or below 55%, with no rounding and no buffer, according to Topstep's consistency article, checked Nov 24, 2026. The same 55% applies to the 50K, 100K and 150K Combines; the dollar "best day recommendation" changes with the profit target.

What happens if my best day is over 55% on a Trading Combine? The account is not failed. Your profit target rises to best day divided by 0.55. Topstep's example: an $1,800 best day on a 50K Combine becomes a $3,273 target, and no later day may exceed $1,800. If a later day makes $2,200, the target becomes $4,000. The remaining profit has to come on a later trading day.

Does a best day of exactly 55% pass the Combine? Topstep's consistency article says the best day must stay at or below 55% of the profit target, so exactly 55% meets that wording. Its Trading Combine parameters page instead says the best day must stay below 55%, and its recommendation table says "less than" the 55% dollar amount. This calculator follows the consistency article and counts exactly 55% as within the limit. Confirm on Topstep's own pages before you trade.

How does the 40% Express Funded Consistency path work? When you activate an Express Funded Account you choose Standard or Consistency. On the Consistency path, your largest single-day net profit must be 40% or less of total net profit, with at least three trading days and at least one trade per day. Exactly 40% qualifies. The calculation resets after a payout request, and the new window starts the next trading day. The Standard path uses five winning days of $150 or more and has no consistency objective.

Do losing days reset my best day? No. Topstep says losses do not reset your best day. If your best day is $1,800 and a losing day brings total profit down to $1,000, the best day is still $1,800, so the percentage goes up.

Can I fix a consistency increase by trading more the same day? No. If today's session set the best day, extra profit today adds to that best day and raises the adjusted target further. Topstep says the remaining profit must be earned on a later trading day, after that session's close. Combine best days lock at 3:10 PM CT. On an Express Funded Consistency path, the dashboard figure locks at 4:00 PM CT.

Does this calculator apply to Topstep Challenges or the Standard XFA path? No. It is for the Trading Combine 55% target and the Express Funded Consistency 40% payout path. The Standard Express Funded path has no consistency objective. This page also does not calculate Live Funded Account or Challenges rules. Topstep's payout policy describes Live Funded payouts with winning-day requirements and does not list a consistency percentage. For Challenges, use Topstep's Challenges article.
