Tradeify Rules (2026): Drawdown, Consistency and Payouts

Updated Nov 13, 2026. Rules verified Nov 13, 2026 from Tradeify's help center: Select evaluation accounts, trailing max drawdowns, consistency rule and Select Flex and Select Daily payout policies. Prop firms change their rules often, so confirm the current version before you trade. Stopline is not affiliated with Tradeify.

The short version

Tradeify's main evaluation is the Select plan. Everyone takes the same evaluation: hit the profit target, never let your balance touch the end-of-day trailing drawdown, and keep your best day at or below 40% of your total profit. There is no daily loss limit during the evaluation. Only after you pass do you pick a payout policy for the funded account: Select Flex (paid every 5 winning days) or Select Daily (daily payouts, with a daily loss limit and a buffer). The choice is permanent for that account.

How the trailing drawdown works

Every Tradeify account uses an end-of-day (EOD) trailing drawdown. The floor follows your highest closing balance and never moves down. It only updates at the close, but it is enforced in real time against your net liquidation value, which includes open trades. If your balance drops to or below the floor at any moment, the account fails, and recovering before the close does not undo it (Tradeify).

When the drawdown stops trailing

Evaluation drawdowns never lock. On sim funded accounts the floor locks at $100 above your starting balance once your closing balance clears it by the drawdown amount, or immediately when you request a payout, whichever comes first (Tradeify). Select Daily uses a smaller drawdown on the 100K and 150K sizes, so its lock comes sooner:

The 40% consistency rule

During the Select evaluation, your biggest single day must be at or below 40% of your total profit. Tradeify is explicit about the edge: a figure exactly at your limit meets the rule, and only a figure strictly above it fails (Tradeify consistency rule). Commissions are not counted in profit, and the percentage updates in real time on the dashboard.

The quick math is biggest day / 0.40. Tradeify's own example: a $1,300 day on a 50K Select needs $3,250 in total profit, not $3,000. Going over does not fail the account. You keep trading smaller winning days until the share drops. Losing days work against you, because they shrink your total while your best day stays the same.

Select funded accounts have no consistency rule. Other Tradeify plans do: Growth sim funded accounts use 35% for payouts, and Lightning funded accounts bought after September 12, 2025 step from 20% on the first payout to 25% and then 30%. The calculator has presets for each.

A simple plan for a 50K Select evaluation

  • Keep each day at or below $1,200, which is 40% of the $3,000 target, so consistency never pushes your target up.
  • Size trades from your room left to the floor, not from the $2,000 headline. The position size calculator does the math.
  • Track your pace with the profit target pace calculator, and log each day in the journal.

FAQ

Does Tradeify Select have a daily loss limit? Not during the evaluation. After you pass, Select Flex also has none, while Select Daily adds one: $500, $1,000, $1,250 or $1,750 depending on account size.

Does exactly 40% pass Tradeify's consistency rule? Yes. Tradeify says any percentage at or below your limit meets the rule, and only a figure strictly above it fails.

Does the Tradeify evaluation drawdown ever lock? No. Only sim funded accounts lock, at $100 above the starting balance, once your closing balance clears the drawdown by $100 or when you request a payout.

Can I pass a Tradeify Select evaluation in one day? No. You need at least 3 trading days because of the 40% consistency rule, or 2 with the 50% consistency add-on.

If my balance dips below the floor and recovers, am I still in? No. The drawdown is enforced in real time on net liquidation value, so touching the floor fails the account even if you finish the day green.
