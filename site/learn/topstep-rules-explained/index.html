Topstep Rules (2026): Maximum Loss Limit and Consistency

Updated Nov 24, 2026. Trading Combine rules were re-checked against Topstep's help center on Nov 24, 2026 (they were previously checked October 16, 2026 and Nov 11, 2026). Topstep also sells Challenges, a separate product that does not replace the Trading Combine; see the note below. Confirm the current version on Topstep's help center before you trade. Stopline is not affiliated with Topstep.

The short version

A Topstep Trading Combine has one rule and two objectives. The rule is to never let your balance touch the Maximum Loss Limit. The objectives are to reach the profit target and to meet the 55% consistency target, according to Topstep's Trading Combine parameters. Get the loss limit wrong and the account is gone. Get consistency wrong and you just have to trade longer.

Loss limits come from Topstep's Maximum Loss Limit article, profit targets from its consistency article and contract limits from the Combine parameters page. You need at least two trading days to pass, and a trading day runs from 5:00 PM CT to 3:10 PM CT the next day.

Topstep Challenges (separate from the Combine)

Checked Nov 24, 2026 against Topstep's Challenges article and the Maximum Loss Limit article. Challenges are a permanent product as of that date. They do not lead to a funded account, and they do not replace or change the Trading Combine rules on this page.

You pay a one-time fee, start at a $0 balance, and have to hit the same profit target twice (a Challenge Round, then a Payout Round) against a static Maximum Loss Limit that does not trail. There is no daily loss limit and no consistency target. Each round lasts up to 90 days. After you take the fixed payout you keep 100% of it, and the account closes. Starter and Launchpad Challenges are the exception on resets: they include two free resets; other Challenges have none.

How the Maximum Loss Limit trails

The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is the lowest your balance is allowed to go. On a 50K Combine you start at $50,000 and the MLL starts $2,000 below, at $48,000. Each time the account closes the day at a new high, the MLL moves up by the same amount. It never moves down. Once it reaches your starting balance it locks there for good (Topstep).

DayEnd-of-day balanceMLL
Start$50,000$48,000
Day 1: +$500$50,500$48,500
Day 2: -$500$50,000$48,500
Later: balance closes at $52,000$52,000$50,000, locked

Two details catch people out. First, the MLL only moves at the end of the day, so a big open profit that you give back before the close does not raise it. Second, the limit is checked in real time against your balance including open trades. Topstep's own example: with the MLL at $48,000, an open trade dips the balance to $47,750, the account is liquidated, and the fill comes back at $48,050. It is still a breach, because the limit was touched first.

Try it in the calculator

Say your best end-of-day balance so far is $51,200 and you are now at $50,800. Your MLL is $51,200 minus $2,000, or $49,200, so you have $1,600 of room. Enter your highest end-of-day balance, not your highest intraday balance, and set the lock to "Starting balance".

The Express Funded Account works differently

After you pass, the Express Funded Account (XFA) starts at a $0 balance. The 50K label describes buying power, not money in the account. The MLL starts at -$2,000, trails up the same way, and locks at $0 once your balance reaches $2,000. After your first payout the MLL is set to $0, so whatever balance is left becomes your cushion (Topstep).

The calculator needs a positive starting balance, so add $50,000 to every number: enter a $50,000 start, and a $700 high becomes $50,700. The room left comes out the same.

The 55% consistency target

In the Combine, your best single day must be no more than 55% of your total profit. The check is best day / total profit. There is no rounding and no buffer (Topstep).

Account sizeProfit targetKeep your best day under
$50K$3,000$1,650
$100K$6,000$3,300
$150K$9,000$4,950

Going over does not fail the account. It raises your target to best day / 0.55. Topstep's example: a $1,800 best day on a 50K means you need $3,273 in total profit instead of $3,000, without any single day topping $1,800. If a later day makes $2,200, that becomes your new best day and the target rises to $4,000. Losing days do not reset your best day.

Timing matters too. Your best day locks at 3:10 PM CT. If you set a new best day today, any extra profit you make today just adds to that best day, so the remaining profit has to come on a later day.

The optional daily loss limit

The Maximum Loss Limit is Topstep's only rule, but Topstep now also offers an optional Daily Loss Limit as part of what it calls the Responsible Trading Advantage. If you add it and hit it, the platform blocks new trades until the next market open. Topstep says this is not a rule break, and in exchange it offers perks such as larger payout caps and discounts on eligible accounts (Topstep). The page we checked does not list the daily limit amounts, so confirm yours on your dashboard. If you use it, size trades so a normal losing day stays well inside it.

XFA payouts: the 40% consistency path

When you activate an XFA you choose a Standard or Consistency payout path. On the Consistency path, your largest single day must be 40% or less of your total net profit, with at least 3 trading days. Topstep's example is $3,600 divided by $9,500, or 37.9%, which qualifies. After you request a payout the count resets to $0 and starts again the next trading day (Topstep).

A simple plan for a 50K Combine

  • Cap each day well under $1,650 so consistency never becomes a problem. Topstep lets you set a Personal Daily Profit Target in TopstepX to help with this.
  • Size trades from the room you have left, not from the $2,000 headline number. Use the position size calculator with your room left as the account risk budget.
  • Track your pace with the profit target pace calculator, and log every day in the journal.
