MyFundedFutures Rapid Plan Rules Explained

Rules checked against the MyFundedFutures help center on October 16, 2026. They change often, so confirm on the MyFundedFutures help center before you trade. Stopline is not affiliated with MyFundedFutures.

The short version

The Rapid plan is built around fast payouts. The evaluation uses an end-of-day drawdown and a consistency rule. Once funded, the consistency rule goes away but the drawdown becomes intraday, which is stricter. There is also a Rapid EOD version that keeps an end-of-day drawdown when funded, in exchange for a tighter consistency rule.

The evaluation drawdown

In the evaluation the max loss is end of day: it is adjusted at the close, and every dollar of new profit raises your minimum balance by a dollar (MyFundedFutures). On a 50K that means you start with a $48,000 floor, and closing a day at $51,200 moves it to $49,200. The help articles we checked do not describe a point where the evaluation drawdown stops trailing, so the safe setting in the calculator is "Never".

The consistency rule

No single day can make up more than 50% of your evaluation profit on Rapid, or 30% on Rapid EOD. MyFundedFutures gives the quick math as profit target / 2, so on a 50K your biggest day should stay at or under $1,500 (MyFundedFutures consistency rule). Using the same math, the 30% rule on Rapid EOD caps your biggest day at $900.

Going over does not breach the account. You simply trade more days until the rule is met. If your best day was $1,800, the total profit that keeps it at 50% is $1,800 divided by 0.5, or $3,600.

The funded account: intraday drawdown and the $100 lock

On a Rapid 50K Sim Funded account, the balance starts at $0. Your max loss sits $2,000 below your equity high-water mark and trails it during the day, open profit included. Once the trailing max loss reaches $100 it locks there for good, and you must always keep at least $100 in the account (MyFundedFutures).

That lock happens when your high-water mark reaches $2,100, which is also the buffer you must build in realized profit before your first payout. After that, Rapid pays out daily, with a $500 minimum request and a 90/10 split. T1 news trading is not allowed on the funded account.

Rapid EOD funded accounts follow the same $100 lock and $2,100 buffer, but the max loss only moves at the close. After the first payout, each new payout unlocks once you make $500 in net profit since the last one (MyFundedFutures).

Using the calculator on a $0 account

The calculator needs a positive starting balance, so add $50,000 to every number and choose "Starting balance + $100". If your equity peaked at $1,400 and you are now at $1,000, enter a $51,400 high and a $51,000 balance. The liquidation level reads $49,400, which is -$600 in real terms, with $1,600 of room. For the intraday version, use your highest equity at any moment, not your best close.

A simple plan for a Rapid 50K

  • In the evaluation, cap each day around $1,500, or $900 on Rapid EOD, so consistency never slows you down.
  • Once funded, remember that open profit you give back still raises your floor until it locks. Taking profits sooner or sizing smaller protects the room you have.
  • Size every trade from your room left with the position size calculator, and keep a journal of each day.
