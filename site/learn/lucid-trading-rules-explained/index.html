Lucid Trading Rules (2026): LucidFlex Drawdown and Consistency

Updated Nov 24, 2026. Rules re-checked Nov 24, 2026 from Lucid Trading's help center: LucidFlex evaluation, LucidFlex drawdown, consistency percentage, customization, funded account and payouts. Prop firms change their rules often, so confirm the current version before you trade. Stopline is not affiliated with Lucid Trading.

The short version

LucidFlex is Lucid Trading's flagship plan. You pay once, take as long as you need, and pass by hitting the profit target with your largest day at 40% or less of your profit. The drawdown is end of day and locks at $100 above your starting balance. At checkout you choose two settings that cannot be changed later: a daily loss limit on or off, and 40% or 50% consistency.

The daily loss limit is your choice

With the daily loss limit on, it applies in both the evaluation and the funded account, the evaluation costs less, and the funded account gets on-demand payouts during the session. With it off, there is no daily cap at all and payouts follow the regular end-of-day schedule. Lucid calls it a soft breach: hit it and you are locked out until the next session, not failed (Lucid). The help articles we checked do not list the daily loss limit amounts, so confirm yours on your dashboard.

How the Max Loss Limit trails and locks

At the end of each session Lucid takes your highest closing balance and raises the Max Loss Limit (MLL) behind it. Once your balance exceeds the Initial Trail Balance, the MLL locks at your starting balance plus $100 and stops moving. Requesting a payout also moves it straight to the locked level. If your balance reaches the MLL, the account is breached (Lucid).

The 40% consistency rule

Your consistency percentage is largest single day profit / account profit, and it must be 40% or less (50% or less with the add-on) to move to a funded account. Exactly 40% therefore meets the rule. Lucid's example: a $750 best day on a $3,000 profit is 25%, which passes (Lucid). Going over does not fail the account. You keep trading until your largest day is back within the limit.

If your best day was $1,300 on a 50K, you need $1,300 / 0.40 = $3,250 in profit. The 50% add-on has a small built-in cushion that Lucid says varies by trader, so the calculator's 40% preset does not cover it. Use Custom at 50% for a conservative read.

Funded LucidFlex and payouts

  • No consistency, no buffer. Funded LucidFlex accounts have no consistency rule and no payout buffer, and the split is 90/10 (Lucid).
  • Five profitable days. Each payout cycle needs 5 days with at least $100 (25K), $150 (50K), $200 (100K) or $250 (150K) of profit, plus positive net profit for the cycle.
  • Size limits. The minimum request is $500. The maximum is 50% of profit up to $1,000, $2,000, $2,500 or $3,000 by account size. Lucid's text says 50% of account balance while its table says 50% of profit, so check your dashboard before you request.
  • Five payouts, then live. You can take up to 5 payouts from each LucidFlex account, after which it moves to live (Lucid payouts).

A simple plan for a 50K LucidFlex

  • Keep each day under $1,200, which is 40% of the $3,000 target, so you can pass in 3 days without the target creeping up.
  • Until the MLL locks at $50,100, size every trade from your room left with the position size calculator.

FAQ

Does LucidFlex have a daily loss limit? Only if you choose it at checkout. With it on, it is a soft breach that locks you out until the next session. The help articles we checked do not list the amounts.

Does exactly 40% pass Lucid's consistency rule? Yes. Lucid says your consistency percentage must be 40% or less, so a best day of exactly 40% of profit meets it.

When does the LucidFlex drawdown lock? Once your balance passes the Initial Trail Balance, for example $52,100 on a 50K, the Max Loss Limit locks at starting balance plus $100. Requesting a payout also locks it.

Is there a consistency rule on funded LucidFlex accounts? No. Consistency applies to the evaluation only.

How many payouts can I take from a LucidFlex account? Up to 5, after which Lucid moves the account to live.
