Take Profit Trader Rules (2026): Drawdown, Consistency and PRO

Updated Nov 24, 2026. Rules verified Nov 24, 2026 from Take Profit Trader's help center: Intraday, Classic and EOD plans, legacy accounts, Rule 1: profit target, Rule 2: position size, Rule 3: drawdown and daily loss limit, Rule 5: consistency, PRO account rules and PRO profit split and withdrawals. Prop firms change their rules often, so confirm the current version before you trade. Stopline is not affiliated with Take Profit Trader.

The short version

Since Nov 23, 2026, new Take Profit Trader (TPT) accounts come in three plans, Intraday, Classic and EOD, at $25K, $50K and $150K. A test account has six rules: hit the profit target, stay within the maximum position size, never touch the trailing drawdown (and, on the EOD plan, respect the daily loss limit), trade approved products during approved hours, be consistent, and never hold counter positions. Pass and you move to a PRO account, where you build a buffer before withdrawing. Accounts bought before Nov 23, 2026 are legacy accounts and keep their original rules, including the $75K and $100K sizes (TPT plans, legacy accounts).

Targets come from Rule 1, drawdowns from Rule 3 and position limits from Rule 2. Micros are allowed at 10 times the contract limit. Targets, drawdowns and contract limits are the same on all three plans. The EOD plan adds a daily loss limit of $500 on 25K, $1,000 on 50K and $2,500 on 150K, counting open P/L and commissions. Hitting it closes your positions and stops trading for the session: if your balance is still at or above your starting balance that is a soft breach and you trade again the next day, and if it is below, the account is breached and needs a reset (TPT Rule 3). The Intraday and Classic plans and legacy accounts have no daily loss limit. PRO+ Development accounts, a live tier TPT assigns after reviewing a trader's risk, have one on every plan (TPT).

How the test drawdown trails

On the Classic and EOD plans and on legacy tests, TPT calculates the test drawdown at the end of the trading day, not during open trades. On the Intraday plan it trails in real time from your peak balance, open profit included, the way PRO does below. Your dashboard calls the floor the Minimum Account Balance. It follows your highest end-of-day balance up and stops for good once it reaches your starting balance. If your account drops to the Minimum Account Balance at any time, through realized or unrealized losses, it is liquidated immediately (TPT Rule 3).

The consistency rule: below 50%, plus 3 days

You need at least 3 trading days, and your best day's net P/L must be below 50% of your total net profit. TPT's wording is "below", so a best day of exactly 50% is not enough. Going over does not fail the test. Your goal rises to more than twice your best day, and the updated number shows in your dashboard (TPT Rule 5).

TPT's example on a 50K: days of +$2,000, +$600 and +$500 make $3,100. The best day is 65% of that, so the goal becomes more than $4,000 ($2,000 x 2), and $2,000 / $4,001 is finally under 50%.

The PRO account works differently

  • Intraday drawdown on most plans. PRO uses the same drawdown amount as the test you passed. On the Intraday and Classic plans and on legacy accounts it trails your peak balance during the day, open profit included. On the EOD plan it stays end of day and keeps the daily loss limit. It still stops at your starting balance (TPT PRO rules). TPT's example: on a 50K PRO, a trade that reaches $1,500 of open profit lifts the floor to $49,500 at once, and if you close it flat you have $500 of room left.
  • Buffer before withdrawals. You can withdraw from day one at an 80/20 split once your balance reaches the buffer, which equals your maximum drawdown: $26,000 on 25K, $52,000 on 50K and $154,500 on 150K. Legacy accounts keep $26,500 on 25K, $77,500 on 75K and $103,000 on 100K (TPT).
  • No consistency rule. No plan has a funded consistency rule, and PRO has a one-time $130 activation fee (takeprofittrader.com, TPT plans).
  • Other PRO rules. A daily profit limit of $10,000 ($5,000 on the EOD plan) pauses trading for the day. Trade at least one day per calendar month, no bots or algos, exit before limit up/down, and be flat from one minute before to one minute after FOMC, NFP and CPI.

PRO+ is the live tier: the drawdown is end of day on the Classic, EOD and legacy plans and intraday on the Intraday plan, with a 90/10 split and no buffer (PRO+ rules, TPT).

A simple plan for a 50K test

  • Keep each day well under half your running profit. A cap near $1,400 keeps you safely below 50% of the $3,000 target.
  • Size from your room left to the Minimum Account Balance using the position size calculator. On the EOD plan, also keep each day's loss inside the $1,000 daily loss limit.
  • Plan at least 3 trading days with the profit target pace calculator.

FAQ

Does Take Profit Trader have a daily loss limit? Only on the EOD plan: $500 on 25K, $1,000 on 50K and $2,500 on 150K, in test, PRO and PRO+. The Intraday and Classic plans and legacy accounts have none, though PRO+ Development accounts have one on every plan.

Is exactly 50% enough for TPT's consistency rule? No. Your best day must be below 50% of total net profit, so the total has to be more than twice your best day.

When does the Take Profit Trader drawdown stop trailing? When the Minimum Account Balance reaches your starting balance. That is true on both test and PRO accounts.

Is the TPT PRO drawdown end of day? Only on the EOD plan. On the Intraday and Classic plans and on legacy accounts, PRO trails intraday from your peak balance, open profit included.

When can I withdraw from a TPT PRO account? Once your balance reaches the buffer, which equals your max drawdown, for example $52,000 on a 50K. Withdrawals are split 80/20 in PRO.
