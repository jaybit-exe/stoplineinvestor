How it works

A journal turns trades into data. Log each profit or loss, plus the amount you risked if you want R multiples (profit divided by risk). Win rate alone says little. Expectancy and profit factor show whether your edge is real.

Entries are saved in this browser. Sign in (optional) to sync them across devices; without an account, clearing your browser data erases them.

If you are trading a prop firm account, you can also see whether your best day breaks consistency in the consistency rule calculator.

FAQ

Where is my journal saved? In your own browser on this device. If you choose to sign in with Google, your journal, XP and streak are also synced so they follow you across devices.

What stats does the journal show? Win rate, total profit or loss, expectancy per trade, profit factor and average R when you log the amount risked, plus an equity curve.

Do I need an account? No. Every tool, including the journal, works without signing in.
