How it works

Divide what is left of your profit target by the trading days you have to get a required daily pace, then compare it with your real average. If the pace needed is far above what you have been making, the plan is asking you to take more risk than usual.

Many challenges also require a minimum number of trading days, so check that as well. A steady, smaller pace that respects your drawdown limit usually beats forcing the target.

Minimum days and consistency

Reaching the number is not always enough. Topstep and MyFundedFutures Rapid need at least 2 trading days, and Tradeify Select and Take Profit Trader tests need at least 3. Most of these evaluations also have a consistency rule, so a single huge day can push the real target higher. Apex evaluations have no minimum days but a 30-calendar-day window.

FAQ

How is the required daily pace calculated? Profit still needed divided by the trading days you have left. If that pace is far above your real daily average, the plan is asking you to take more risk than usual.

Does hitting the profit target pass the evaluation? Only if you also meet the other rules. Depending on the firm that means a minimum number of trading days, a consistency rule, and never touching the trailing drawdown.

Which firms have no minimum trading days? Of the firms preset here, Apex evaluations have no minimum and can pass in one day, within a 30-calendar-day window.
